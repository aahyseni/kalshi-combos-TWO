## The mechanism (no knobs — every number derived or measured)

For a combo where ONE player's legs span the whole combo (all legs DNP-able
single-named-player props, one extractable entity; UNKNOWN entity extraction
FAILS CLOSED into scope):

1. **VOID-BRANCH PRICING** (`PricingEngine._price_suffix`): fair =
   `(1−h)·V_corr + h·⌊∏s⌋`, applied only when `⌊∏s⌋ > V_corr` (Δ > 0 — the
   correction can only RAISE our ask, mirroring UNKNOWN→widen). `h` = the
   MEASURED per-family DNP hazard from our own settled-leg corpus:
   - baseline table (`pricing/dnp_scalar.BASELINE_DNP_LEG_RESULTS`, counts not
     rates) regenerated by `tools/measure_dnp_hazard.py` from the 2026-08-06
     exchange-truth pull — **997 finalized single-player prop legs, 23 scalar
     = pooled 2.31%** (HIT 6/214=2.80%, HR 3/67=4.48%, HRR 6/288=2.08%,
     KS 7/383=1.83%, TB 1/14=7.14%, OUTS 0/19, RBI 0/10, SB 0/2; the
     forensics' 978/2.35% is the same corpus minus OUTS);
   - **live refresh on the settlement cadence**: the settled-leg cache now
     records graded scalars, and the lifecycle merges the session's counts
     onto the baseline whenever a new graded outcome lands
     (`lifecycle._maybe_refresh_dnp_hazards` → `engine.set_dnp_hazard_counts`);
   - **thinness rule (derived, not tuned):** a family's own rate is used only
     when its sample ≥ `1/pooled` legs (the size at which one scalar is
     expected at the pooled rate — absence becomes informative); a THIN family
     fails closed to `max(own, pooled)` (TB 1/14 → keeps its 7.14%, OUTS 0/19
     → lifts to pooled — never below pooled on an untrustable sample, never
     below its own observation either); a combo takes the MAX over its leg
     families (a scratch is a player-level event; underestimating h
     under-prices the void branch). Empty corpus ⇒ no mixture — safe, because:
2. **SNIPER ROBUSTNESS FLOOR** (`construct_quote(dnp_ask_floor_cc=…)`): the
   taker who KNOWS the scratch has h = 1 and no base-rate mixture stops them.
   The implied YES ask (`$1 − no_bid`) of an in-scope combo is FLOORED at
   `⌊∏s⌋` — the exact DNP settlement value — by clamping `no_raw` down before
   the free-money clamp and grid snap (both of which also only ever lower
   `no_bid`, so the floor survives them). **A DNP-informed taker pays ≥ what
   the combo settles at: we either profit or don't lose on the scratch.** The
   clamp self-triggers exactly when Δ exceeds the quote's total margin
   (fair + margin < ⌊∏s⌋); every small-Δ combo is untouched byte-identically.
